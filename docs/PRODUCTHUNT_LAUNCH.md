## 🚫 Don'ts

- **Don't ask people to upvote in DMs explicitly** — PH detects and de-ranks. Say "going live today, would love your eyes on it."
- **Don't post unfinished images** — PH judges first impressions on the gallery thumbnail.
- **Don't reply to comments with the same template twice** — readers see it and bounce.
- **Don't pin the launch on your X profile until you have momentum** — empty replies look like a bot.
