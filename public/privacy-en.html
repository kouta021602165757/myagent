Article 5 (Handling of Chat Content)

  1. Chat content between users and AI on the Service is sent to Anthropic's API and stored in our database.
  2. We use chat content only for providing, improving, and detecting fraud in the Service. We do not sell or publish chat content to third parties.
  3. Users can delete chat history anytime from the "Data" tab in account settings.

Article 5-2 (Information Sharing in Group Chat)

  1. When you join a group chat, your messages, name, avatar, and join timestamp are visible to other members.
  2. AI invocations in group chat are billed to the host (group creator). Usage history (who called the AI when) is disclosed to the host.
  3. If you leave a group, your prior messages remain in other members' history. To request full deletion, ask the host.

Article 5-3 (Send-to-group Feature)

  1. "Send to group" is a transfer of credit balance within the Service, not a transfer of cash or financial assets.
  2. Refunds after sending are generally not accepted.
  3. Send records are stored in both parties' transaction history.

Article 6 (Cookies)

The Service uses cookies and localStorage to maintain authentication state, analyze usage, etc. If you refuse cookies, some features (such as staying signed in) may not work. For users in the EU/UK, we display a consent banner on first access and do not place analytics cookies without consent.

Article 7 (Disclosure / Correction / Deletion of Personal Info)

  1. Users may request disclosure, correction, addition, or deletion of their personal information held by us.
  2. To make a request, contact kota.takeuchi@protocol.ooo. We will verify your identity and respond within a reasonable period.
  3. You can also delete your account from "Settings → Delete account" inside the app. After deletion, data cannot be recovered.
